## ARMOR

| Armor | AC | Cost | Weight | Stealth | Strength | Proficiency |
|-------|----|----|--------|---------|----------|------------|
| **Padded** | 11 + DEX | 5 gp | 8 lb | Disadvantage | — | Light |
| **Leather** | 11 + DEX | 10 gp | 10 lb | — | — | Light |
| **Studded Leather** | 12 + DEX | 45 gp | 13 lb | — | — | Light |

**Light Armor Features:**
- You add your full Dexterity modifier to the base AC
- You can move at full speed

- Best for: Rogues, Monks, Rangers, some Clerics

| **Ring Mail** | 14 | 30 gp | 40 lb | Disadvantage | — | 10 min/5 min |

| **Splint** | 17 | 200 gp | 60 lb | Disadvantage | STR 15 | 10 min/5 min |
| **Plate** | 18 | 1,500 gp | 65 lb | Disadvantage | STR 15 | 10 min/5 min |

**Heavy Armor Features:**
- You do NOT add your Dexterity modifier to AC
- Your base AC is fixed (not calculated)
- Provides maximum protection
- Imposes Stealth disadvantage
- Some require Strength minimum (speed reduced 10 ft if not met)
- Best for: Fighters, Paladins, heavy-armor Clerics
- Don/Doff Time: Don = putting on armor, Doff = taking it off

### SHIELDS

| Shield | AC Bonus | Cost | Weight | Type |
|--------|----------|------|--------|------|
| **Shield** | +2 | 10 gp | 6 lb | Standard |
| **Shield, Tower** | +2* | 20 gp | 15 lb | Heavy (see notes) |

**Shield Rules:**
- A shield increases your AC by 2 (in addition to your armor AC)
- You can only benefit from one shield at a time
- You must have a free hand to use a shield
- Shield of Faith spell also grants +2 AC (doesn't stack with physical shield)

**Tower Shield (Special):**
- You can take a special action to gain three-quarters cover while holding it
- While doing so, you lose the ability to attack and your movement is halved

### SPECIAL ARMOR MATERIALS

#### Mithral
- Cost: +800 gp
- Effect: Doesn't impose Strength requirement, doesn't impose Stealth disadvantage
- Best for: Medium/Heavy armor users who need Stealth

#### Adamantine
- Cost: +500 gp  
- Effect: Critical hits against you are treated as normal hits
- Best for: Defense-focused characters

#### Silvered
- Cost: +100 gp or +25% (whichever is higher)
- Effect: Overcomes resistance to non-magical damage from certain creatures (especially lycanthropes)

## SPECIAL RULES

### ARMOR PROFICIENCY

**Who has it:**
- **Light Armor:** Barbarians, Bards, Clerics, Druids, Monks, Rogues, Sorcerers, Warlocks, Wizards
- **Medium Armor:** Barbarians, Clerics, Druids, Fighters, Monks (Monks only with specific subclass), Paladins, Rangers
- **Heavy Armor:** Clerics (specific domains), Fighters, Paladins
- **Shields:** Barbarians, Bards, Clerics, Fighters, Paladins, Rangers, Warlocks (specific pacts)

**Penalties for non-proficiency:**
- Disadvantage on ability checks, saving throws, and attack rolls involving STR or DEX
- Cannot cast spells while wearing armor you're not proficient with

### DONNING AND DOFFING ARMOR

| Armor Type | Don | Doff |
|-----------|-----|------|
| Light | 1 minute | 1 minute |
| Medium | 5 minutes | 1 minute |
| Heavy | 10 minutes | 5 minutes |
| Shield | 1 action | 1 action |

**Important:** You only benefit from armor's AC if you take the full time to don it properly.

---

### ARMOR CLASS CALCULATION

#### No Armor (10 + DEX)
- Base AC of 10 plus your Dexterity modifier
- Example: AC 10 + 2 (DEX) = AC 12

#### Light Armor (Armor AC + Full DEX)
- Example: Leather AC 11 + 3 (DEX) = AC 14
- Best for high DEX characters

#### Medium Armor (Armor AC + DEX max +2)
- Example: Breastplate AC 14 + 2 (DEX) = AC 16 (even with +3 DEX)
- Example: Chain Shirt AC 13 + 1 (DEX) = AC 14
- Best for characters with DEX 14-16

#### Heavy Armor (Armor AC only)
- Example: Plate AC 18
- No DEX bonus at all
- Best for STR-based characters
- Can use negative DEX modifier without penalty

#### Shield (+2)
- Adds to any armor AC calculation
- Example: Plate (18) + Shield (+2) = AC 20
- Can only have one shield at a time

---

### STRENGTH REQUIREMENTS

Some heavy armor requires minimum Strength to use effectively:
- **STR 13 Required:** Chain Mail
- **STR 15 Required:** Splint, Plate

**Penalty for not meeting requirement:** Your speed is reduced by 10 feet.

---

### STEALTH DISADVANTAGE

The following armor types impose disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks:
- **Light:** Padded
- **Medium:** Hide, Scale Mail, Half Plate
- **Heavy:** Ring Mail, Chain Mail, Splint, Plate

---

### ENCUMBRANCE (VARIANT RULE)

Standard carrying capacity: **STR score x 15 lbs**
- Example: STR 16 = can carry up to 240 lbs

**Encumbered:** Over 5x STR score
- Speed reduced by 10 feet
- Disadvantage on ability checks using STR or DEX

**Heavily Encumbered:** Over 10x STR score
- Speed reduced by 20 feet
- Disadvantage on STR and DEX attack rolls and saving throws

## IMPORTANT NOTES

- **Armor Stacking:** You can only use one armor AC calculation at a time (can't wear armor AND use Unarmored Defense)
- **Proficiency Bonus:** Only added to attack rolls, not damage
- **Shields:** Don't provide AC if you're not proficient, but penalties are reduced (no disadvantage, just lose the +2)
- **Magic Items:** Can modify or enhance any of these equipment options
- **Mounts:** Have their own carrying capacity (varies by creature)
- **Bonus Actions:** Donning/doffing a shield uses 1 action, not a bonus action
- **Starting Equipment:** Choose based on class recommendations or take gold and buy items from these lists
