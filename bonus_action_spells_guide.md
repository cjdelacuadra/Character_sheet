# D&D 5E BONUS ACTION SPELLS GUIDE
## Spells You Can Cast As Bonus Action + Attack With Action

---

## THE BONUS ACTION + ATTACK COMBO RULE

**THE KEY RULE:**
When you cast a spell as a bonus action, you can still use your action to make weapon attacks, attack rolls with cantrips, or use class features that grant attacks.

**WHAT YOU CANNOT DO:**
If you cast any spell as a bonus action, you cannot cast another spell with your action UNLESS that spell is a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action.

**Example Combinations That WORK:**
- ✅ Cast Hunter's Mark (bonus action) + Attack with sword (action)
- ✅ Cast Misty Step (bonus action) + Attack with hand crossbow (action) 
- ✅ Cast Spiritual Weapon (bonus action to cast, then bonus to attack) + Attack with melee weapon (action)
- ✅ Cast Hex (bonus action) + Fire Bolt cantrip (action)

**Example Combinations That DON'T WORK:**
- ❌ Cast Hunter's Mark (bonus action) + Cast Fireball (action)
- ❌ Cast Misty Step (bonus action) + Cast Shield (reaction, if you already cast a leveled spell this turn)
- ❌ Cast Shillelagh (bonus action) + Cast another leveled spell (action)

## HOW TO USE BONUS ACTION SPELLS EFFECTIVELY

### STEP-BY-STEP TURN EXAMPLE

**Turn Sequence with Bonus Action Spell:**

1. **Use Bonus Action:** Cast bonus action spell (e.g., Hunter's Mark on goblin)
2. **Move:** Move up to your movement speed (e.g., 30 ft)
3. **Use Action:** Make weapon attacks (Attack action)
   - Attack with sword (add Hunter's Mark damage if hit)
   - Attack with second weapon if you have Two-Weapon Fighting
   - Cantrip with attack roll (Fire Bolt, etc.)
4. **Use Reaction:** React to enemy attacks/triggers (Shield spell, opportunity attack, etc.)

**Example: Ranger with Hunter's Mark**
- Bonus Action: Cast Hunter's Mark on goblin (1d6 extra damage when you hit)
- Move 30 ft closer to goblin
- Action: Attack twice with longbow (with Hunter's Mark bonus)
- Reaction: Ready to shoot if goblin moves away

## RESTRICTIONS & LIMITATIONS

### THE BONUS ACTION SPELL RULE

**If you cast a spell as a bonus action, you can ONLY:**
- Make weapon attacks with your action
- Cast a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action
- Use class features that grant attacks (Divine Smite, Sneak Attack, etc.)
- Use your movement and interact with objects normally

**You CANNOT:**
- Cast another spell with your action (even a different spell)
- Ready another spell to cast later (if you have bonus action spell this turn)
- Cast a reaction spell if you already cast a non-cantrip spell this turn (RAW—many DMs ignore this)

### CONCENTRATION LIMITS

**Spells that require concentration:**
- You can only maintain one concentration spell at a time
- If you cast another concentration spell, the first one ends
- If you get hit while concentrating, you must make a save to maintain concentration

**Best non-concentration bonus action spells:**
- Misty Step
- Spiritual Weapon
- Healing Word
- Magic Stone
- Shield of Faith (buffer is okay)
- Divine Word

## IMPORTANT NOTES

1. **Quickened Spell** - Sorcerers can spend 2 Sorcery Points to cast any spell as a bonus action
2. **War Caster** - Allows spellcasting while holding weapons/shields
3. **Bonus Actions ARE Limited** - You can only use ONE bonus action per turn (no stacking)
4. **Movement is Flexible** - You can move before/after/between actions
5. **Reactions are Separate** - Reaction spells (Shield, Counterspell, etc.) don't count as your action/bonus action
